## Investigation Framework

One framework I consistently practice throughout these lessons:

1. Clarify the business question.
2. Validate the metric and underlying data.
3. Decompose the KPI into its drivers.
4. Segment the affected population.
5. Form hypotheses.
6. Test the strongest explanations.
7. Quantify the business impact.
8. Recommend an action.

The goal is to think like a senior analyst rather than simply write SQL or calculate a metric.

---

## Technical Interview Framework

Before writing SQL or Python:

1. Define what one output row should represent.
2. Understand the grain of the source data.
3. Identify joins, filters, and possible duplication.
4. Break the solution into logical steps.
5. Write the simplest correct implementation.
6. Check nulls, edge cases, and observation windows.
7. Validate that the result answers the business question.
8. Explain the business meaning of the output.

## Why I Built This Repository

I wanted a single place to document what I'm learning while preparing for Senior Analytics interviews. Rather than memorizing SQL syntax or statistical definitions in isolation, I'm focusing on developing the analytical judgment, technical fluency, and business communication needed to solve real problems.

The exercises increasingly combine SQL, Python, statistics, experimentation, and business reasoning because real analytics interviews rarely test those skills in isolation.

This repository serves as both a personal knowledge base and a public record of my continued learning and interview preparation.
